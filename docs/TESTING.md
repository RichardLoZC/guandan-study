# Verification

Run `npm test` before packaging. Tests cover physical card conservation, valid moves, tribute and exemption, review reconstruction, save failures, model-response validation, host/guest state, reconnection, four-human rooms, localization and phone hand projections.

UI acceptance covers Chinese/English switching, selection retention, real model advice, flat/fan hand layouts and phone portrait/landscape layouts. English teaching summaries use actual move data and explicitly distinguish model ranking from explanatory text. The 3D renderer and its graphics-specific tests were removed in 1.8.6.

macOS packaging includes a strict code-signature integrity check. Android packaging checks the APK signature. The `Windows package` workflow builds a ZIP on Windows, extracts it, compares bundled resources and model hashes to source, and launches the shipped EXE in Electron's Node CLI mode to check the runtime, rules, localization and portable storage. This is separate from native Windows graphical gameplay testing. Responsive browser checks do not establish performance on every phone. Tablet support shares the same Android package.

`.gitattributes` preserves upstream bundle bytes on Windows. Git's automatic CRLF conversion caused the initial provenance-hash test failure; the test remains strict and the original hashes are unchanged.

The optional `test:mac` script is an existing developer smoke harness; it uses isolated data. UI screenshots and internal reports under `test-results/` are deliberately excluded from publication.

Hand tools (1.8.7; placement updated in 1.8.12): Pass/Play are prominent in the fixed bottom dock, with arrangement tools on the left and teaching/history on the right. Test priority overlap (flush versus bomb), missing targets, physical card conservation and undo invalidation after a play. Verify Chinese/English and narrow windows.

Straight-flush rules (1.8.9): a five-card straight with one natural suit, including wild substitutions, must be played as a straight flush. Verify that recognition, worker policy inputs, teaching decomposition and LAN commits agree. A mixed-suit alternative using another physical card must remain available. Existing saved actions retain their historical interpretation; new actions cannot reuse the obsolete ordinary-straight key. The DanLM weights are unchanged.

Saved hand groups (1.8.10): drag multiple selected cards onto a shelf; the moved selection clears and the cards form a separate group with a Play group button. Verify flat and fan views, in-place grouping, multiple groups, undo, reload, partial removal and full group play. The button must validate current group membership, turn, phase and beating rules again on click, and use the existing LAN play command. An invalid/stale group must never submit a pass. Automatic grouping and rank reset keep their existing selection behavior.

Round table and group colors (1.8.11): a finished round retains the felt, all three players and their latest trick actions. Remaining hands are revealed at their rotated seat; own remaining cards stay below the result strip. Verify single/double finishes, zero/27-card remainders, next-round reset, English, flat/fan layouts and Pad landscape/portrait. All four LAN perspectives are tested for card conservation and active-round secrecy.

Group rails sit above the cards. Since 1.8.12, each shelf reserves the complete rotated card bounds; no card face is clipped to make room for the next shelf. Color is stable by combination type: pairs/triples green, runs/full houses blue, 4–5-card bombs purple, straight flushes and 6-card bombs orange, larger bombs/four jokers gold. This is a visual hierarchy, not a measured probability. The common gaming palette follows [Epic's rarity convention](https://www.fortnite.com/news/v3-4-content-update?lang=en-US). Subtle shine starts at blue; gold adds a slow glow. Reduced-motion settings disable these effects. Short windows scroll the table independently; the bottom dock remains visible.

Check four simultaneous plays, latest-play emphasis, tribute, long combinations, end-of-round remainders, and English. Group labels appear beside their Play group action; the fixed-size Arrange button contains the plan counter, so cycling does not move controls. Pass/Play sit in a dedicated footer outside the scrolling table. Since 1.8.18, empty shelves reserve the same height as occupied shelves. Test both exposed corners of a 27-card upper fan with real pointer clicks, moving the selected end cards to the lower shelf, and switching flat/fan without changing selection. Geometry regression checks every count from 1 to 27, four card sizes, group gaps, and selection lift against the complete shelf bounds.

Shared card size (1.8.13): table plays and hand cards use the same width and height at each responsive breakpoint (desktop 72 × 108 CSS px). Latest play uses a stronger seat-colored frame. Verify flat/fan layouts, long plays, two shelves and tablet sizes. Long table plays reduce overlap spacing to fit their seat without resizing individual cards.

Hand preparation and search (1.8.18): selection and arrangement remain available off-turn, paused, after model errors and during LAN requests. Submission remains turn- and rule-gated. Verify AI commits retain preselection and do not detach card DOM during a drag. Late LAN responses must preserve newer selections and selections in a subsequent round, even when physical IDs repeat. Leaving an unstarted lobby must rebuild the card-node map. Search tests cover full physical-card partitions, mixed-suit choices, two identical straight flushes, wild substitution freeing natural bombs, priorities and input immutability. Compare hand, shelf, public-table and dock rectangles before/after the first move to the empty upper row in flat/fan layouts and phone portrait/landscape.

Mouse box selection (1.8.19): blank-space mouse drags select cards without moving them. Window/crossing selection uses transformed image faces after occlusion and viewport clipping; test rotated bounding-box corners, both shelves, scale, edge contact, hidden cards and non-overlay scrollbars. Shift adds, Escape restores the original selection, and interrupted gestures never commit. New rounds, layouts, dialogs, scroll, resize, blur and visibility changes cancel the box. A fresh mouse/touch/pen press clears trailing-click suppression. Touch scrolling and card reordering remain separate. Real UI checks include five-card flat selection, Shift adding two, curved upper-row selection, a partial hit on the curved endpoint, 27-card two-row preview, and Escape cancellation.

Selection cancellation (1.8.20): only stationary primary clicks/taps on known empty table/hand surfaces clear the selection. Validate down/move/up/click ownership, movement out and back, release-distance checks, touch scrolling, wheels, interruptions, multi-touch, modifiers, scrollbars and stale layout/selection snapshots. Escape cancels the active gesture before clearing selection, ignores repeats/IME/editable fields, and belongs to open dialogs. A cancelled held gesture suppresses its eventual click until the next physical primary press, without a timeout. Shift-click adds a displayed-order range, invalidating its anchor after a reorder, new round or externally changed selection. Tests use real hand-preparation gates; no game move is committed by selection.
